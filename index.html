Student Pickup

Safe school pickup with Guestrar. Parents verify by OTP and add pickup persons; every person gets a separate QR pass for each child. Security scans the pass, the Pickup Display inside the school shows who is waiting, and staff mark the handover.

Working prototype

Clickable end-to-end demo: school admin web, parent portal, security app and the Pickup Display, all connected.

Parent portal flow

Every parent and pickup-person screen step by step, with the flow chart and problem cases.
  1. Open the working prototype and click Share on WhatsApp.
  2. Tap the link in the WhatsApp group, enter 9876500001, OTP 1234.
  3. Create the profile and add a driver.
  4. On the security phone tap the scanner icon, scan the driver's pass, Confirm pickup.
  5. On the Pickup Display tap Sending, then Handed over.

Demo with sample data only. No real SMS, OTP or backend. Requires an internet connection.
